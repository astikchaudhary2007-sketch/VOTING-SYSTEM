How online voting works

  1. Enrolment

    Submit your personal details, EPIC number and constituency. Applicants must be 18 or older on the date of poll.

  2. Authentication

    Sign in with your EPIC number and password, then enter the six-digit OTP sent to your registered mobile.

  3. Ballot

    The ballot unit lists every candidate in your constituency with party name and symbol. Select one and confirm.

  4. Receipt

    A unique receipt number is issued. It proves your ballot was counted without revealing who you voted for.

Eligibility & guidelines

  • You must be a citizen aged 18 years or above on the date of poll.
  • You may vote only in the assembly constituency where you are enrolled.
  • Each elector has one vote. A vote cannot be changed once it is cast.
  • If you do not wish to vote for any candidate, you may choose NOTA (None of the Above).
  • A voting session expires after 10 minutes of sign-in for your security.
  • Five incorrect password attempts lock the account for 15 minutes.
  • Never share your password or OTP. Officials will never ask for them.

Frequently asked questions

Is my vote secret?
Yes. When you cast a vote, the system marks your name on the voter roll as "voted" and places your ballot in a separate ballot box. The ballot holds only the constituency, your choice and a rounded timestamp — never your EPIC number. Ballots are also stored in random order.
What is the receipt number for?
The receipt number (format EB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) lets you check on the Verify Receipt page that your ballot is present in the ballot box. It does not show which candidate you selected.
Can I change my vote after submitting?
No. As with a physical ballot, a vote is final once it is cast. You are shown a confirmation screen before submission so you can review your choice.
I did not receive my OTP.
The OTP is valid for two minutes. Use the "Resend OTP" option on the sign-in page. In this demonstration the OTP is displayed on screen as a simulated SMS.
When will results be declared?
Counting takes place on the scheduled counting date. Provisional figures are visible on the Results page whenever the Returning Officer has published them.
What does NOTA mean?
"None of the Above" lets you formally reject all candidates. NOTA votes are counted and shown in the results, but NOTA cannot win a seat; the candidate with the most votes is still elected.
