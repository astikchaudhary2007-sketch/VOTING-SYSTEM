Verify your ballot receipt

Enter the receipt number you received after voting to confirm that your ballot is present in the ballot box and will be counted.

What this check tells you

  • That a ballot with this receipt number exists in the ballot box.
  • The constituency it was cast in and the time (to the nearest minute).
  • It does not show the candidate selected — this protects ballot secrecy, so a receipt cannot be used to prove how someone voted.
